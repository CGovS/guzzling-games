Profile

Counsel where policy meets the boardroom

Charles W. Johnson IV represents clients before Congress and the executive branch on a diverse set of policy matters, with an emphasis on automotive, congressional investigations, energy, environmental and international trade issues.

With more than three decades of legal, policy and advocacy experience in Washington, Charlie provides strategic counsel on major corporate transactions as federal policymakers review their potential legal, regulatory and policy implications. He pairs a lawyer's command of the substance with a practitioner's feel for how decisions are actually made on Capitol Hill and across the agencies.

He serves as the Hiring Partner for Akin Gump's Washington, D.C. office, where he has practiced since 1994.
